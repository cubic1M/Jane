# Strategy results

Every strategy in [the catalog](strategy-catalog.md), replayed on Alpaca SIP daily closes, 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-23, after costs. A replay of the past, not a forecast; practice money and demo advice only.

- **Walk-forward:** each year from 2020 is scored with the setting (and, for whole portfolios, the blend with bills that matches the level's volatility) chosen by Sortino on the years before it.
- **Several start years:** the walk-forward must also beat the mix measured from 2021 and 2022.
- **Fixed split:** chosen on the years to 2021-12-31, scored from 2022.
- **The haircut:** 74 trials were tested (every strategy at every setting). The deflated Sharpe ratio (Bailey & López de Prado, 2014) of each walk-forward's daily excess over the level's mix, against the best Sharpe ratio that many trials would show by luck, is the chance its edge is real.
- **Recommendable** only with that chance at least 95%, more return for its risk than the mix both since 2020 and from 2022, and no riskier than the level's budget or its own mix.
